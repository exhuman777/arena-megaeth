ABOUT ARENA SURVIVAL

⚔️ The Game

Arena Survival is a roguelike survival game where you fight endless waves of monsters in a brutal arena. Each run is unique - collect loot, build combos, and climb the leaderboard. Death is permanent for each run, but your warrior grows stronger over time through persistent upgrades and equipment.

🛠️ Technology

Built as a browser-based game with a Node.js backend for persistent data. No downloads required - play instantly in your browser.

JavaScript HTML5 Canvas ROT.js Node.js Express SQLite JWT Auth

🎮 How to Play

Use WASD or Arrow keys to move. Attack enemies by walking into them or using attack keys (H/X). Use AOE attacks (J/C) to hit multiple enemies at once. Collect loot, survive as long as possible, and beat the high score!

🎯 Game Features

  • Wave System - Progressively harder enemy waves
  • Combo Kills - Chain kills for bonus XP and epic titles
  • Chain Lightning - Critical hits bounce between enemies
  • Biome Changes - Arena transforms as you play
  • Training System - Permanently upgrade your stats
  • Shop - Buy weapons, armor, and potions
  • Achievements - Complete challenges for gold
  • Inventory - Manage and equip your gear
