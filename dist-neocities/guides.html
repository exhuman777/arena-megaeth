📖 HOW TO PLAY

Master the arena and become a legend

🎮 Controls

WASD LAYOUT

WASD Move your warrior
H Attack nearby enemy
J AOE attack (area damage)
K Pick up items

ARROW LAYOUT

↑↓←→ Move your warrior
X Attack nearby enemy
C AOE attack (area damage)
V Pick up items
SPACE Attack (works with both layouts) / Restart after death
P Pause game

⚔️ Gameplay Basics

Survive endless waves of monsters in the arena. Each wave brings stronger enemies.

TIP: Walking into an enemy automatically attacks them. Use attack keys for quick strikes without moving.

Combat System

  • Critical Hits - Deal extra damage with a chance on each attack
  • Combo System - Kill enemies quickly to build combos for bonus XP
  • Chain Lightning - Critical hits can chain to nearby enemies
  • AOE Attack - Damages all enemies within 2 tiles (3 second cooldown)

Combo Milestones

Build combos by killing enemies quickly:

  • 5x - RAMPAGE
  • 10x - KILLING SPREE
  • 15x - DOMINATING
  • 20x - GODLIKE
  • 30x - LEGENDARY

📈 Progression

Your warrior persists between games:

  • Level Up - Gain XP from kills. Higher combos = more XP
  • Gold - Earned from kills and achievements
  • Training - Spend gold to permanently upgrade HP, Damage, Defense
  • Achievements - Complete challenges for gold rewards
  • Inventory - Keep items between games, equip your best gear

🧠 Pro Tips

  • 1 Kite enemies by moving away while attacking. Don't let them surround you.
  • 2 Save AOE attack for when multiple enemies cluster together.
  • 3 Prioritize killing weaker enemies to build combo before fighting toughs.
  • 4 Pick up health potions immediately when low HP.
  • 5 Invest gold in Training upgrades - they're permanent.
  • 6 Use arena corners to avoid being surrounded.
  • 7 Critical chain lightning can clear groups - maximize crit chance!
