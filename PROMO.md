### What is it?

Fight endless waves of monsters. Get the highest score. Win real prizes.

Every game costs 0.001 ETH to play. 95% of all entry fees go into a daily prize pool. At the end of each day, whoever has the top score wins the entire pool.

### Why MegaETH?

Traditional blockchains are too slow for real gaming. You'd submit a score and wait 15 seconds... 30 seconds... hoping it confirms. That kills the experience.

MegaETH has 10ms block times. When you die in Arena Survival, your score hits the blockchain before you can blink. It feels like a normal game, except everything is provably fair and on-chain.

This is what the future of blockchain gaming looks like.

### The Tech

- Scores stored permanently on MegaETH
- Smart contract handles prize pools automatically
- No backend servers for game logic - just you vs the chain
- Pay-before-play prevents score manipulation
